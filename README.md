# Music Visualizer 

i made a simple 3D music visualizer that reacts to the music you play.

Upload a song from your computer and watch the 3D bars move based on the audio frequencies.....

## Features

* you can upload and play your own music
* it gives real time audio visualization
* it also form 3d shape
* Bars change height according to the music beats
* Dynamic colors based on audio energy
* 3D shaped also float 
* you can also move float and rotate manually
* Auto rotate camera option

## Tech Stack

* HTML
* CSS
* JavaScript


## How It Works

The selected audio is processed using the Web Audio API.....

The frequency data from the song is then used to control the height of the 3D bars....
overall energy and different frequency ranges affect the visualizer while the song is playing.



## Controls

* Upload a song using the file picker
* Use the audio player to play or pause
* Drag around the visualizer to rotate the camera
* Scroll to zoom
* Use the Camera panel to enable auto rotation or change the camera position
